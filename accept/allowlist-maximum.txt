# open_lane.knowledge in the pipeline). These are the legacy lane's rules for groups of inputs on networks
# beyond linear maps, supplied as operations rather than found: proved sound and priced as cost-v3 prices the
# leaf. The minimal-knowledge condition does not have them; a checker that uses one there is refused scoring.
#
# The interval rule on a general network: CostM.ginterval, soundness CostM.ginterval_correct (23 October 2026).
CompactCheck.Interp.ginterval
#
# The token_interval rule on a general network, for a list of leaves: CostM.gtokenIntervals, soundness
# CostM.gtokenIntervals_correct (23 October 2026). The rows that depend on their token alone are charged once
# per call, as cost-v3 charges them once per proof.
CompactCheck.Interp.gtokenIntervals
